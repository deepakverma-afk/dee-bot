deeBOT

A simple trivia bot built for slack

What it does

deeBOT lets you play trivia directly in Slack, track your score and compete on a leaderboard.

Commands

/dee-trivia
/dee-answer A
/dee-score
/dee-leaderboard
/dee-help

Built with

JavaScript · Node.js · Slack Bolt · Open Trivia DB · Hack Club Nest
